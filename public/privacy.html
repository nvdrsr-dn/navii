Privacy Policy for Navii AI Companion

Effective Date: September 27, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Navii AI ("we", "our", or "the Application") collects, uses, and protects information when interacting with users through the web application and the official Meta Instagram Messaging API.

1. Information We Process

When you interact with Navii via Instagram Direct Messages or the web interface, we process:

  • Direct Messages: The text content of messages sent to Navii to generate conversational AI responses.
  • User Identifiers: Your scoped Instagram User ID (IGSID) provided by Meta to deliver messages back to your chat thread.
  • Conversational Context: Temporary recent conversation history to provide natural conversational memory and emotional continuity.

2. How We Use Information

The information collected is used exclusively for:

  • Processing and responding to your conversational messages in real time.
  • Maintaining conversational context and continuity across active chat sessions.
  • Providing dynamic typing indicators and human-paced responses.

We do NOT sell, rent, monetize, or share your personal information or messages with third-party advertisers or data brokers.

3. Third-Party Services & Meta API Compliance

Navii operates in strict compliance with the Meta Platform Terms and Developer Policies:

  • Meta Instagram Graph API: Used solely to receive webhook notifications and deliver direct messages requested by users.
  • AI Model Inference: Message text is processed using enterprise LLM APIs (such as Google Gemini / OpenRouter) solely for text generation. These providers do not use your private API inputs to train public foundation models.

4. Data Retention and Security

We do not store passwords, credit cards, or sensitive identity documents. Conversation logs and temporary session tokens are stored securely in protected backend environments and rotated regularly.

5. User Data Deletion Instructions

Users have the right to request the deletion of any stored conversation logs or session identifiers. To request data deletion:

  • Visit our Data Deletion Instructions Page.
  • Or send a message saying "Forget our conversation" or "Clear memory" directly in chat, which automatically purges your session memory.
  • Alternatively, email your deletion request to: dhruvsagar693@gmail.com.
